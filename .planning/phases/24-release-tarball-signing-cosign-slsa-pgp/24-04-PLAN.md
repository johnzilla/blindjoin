<objective>
Add a one-paragraph cross-reference to `docs/RELEASING.md` at the END of the existing `## Tagging releases` section in `CONTRIBUTING.md` (D-11 + D-20). The new paragraph names the post-tag maintainer-side procedure (PGP sign on YubiKey, .asc upload, draft flip) and includes an audience-disambiguation lede ("Most contributors don't need it") so contributors reading the section know not to follow the cross-ref unless they're cutting a release.

Purpose: This is the operator-facing entry point into docs/RELEASING.md for contributors who reach CONTRIBUTING.md first. Without it, the maintainer-side procedure is undiscoverable from the contributor manual — contributors who happen to be the maintainer doing a release have no signal that docs/RELEASING.md exists. The audience-disambiguation lede prevents non-maintainer contributors from going down the release-engineering rabbit hole (D-11 audience separation principle).

Output: One modified file: `CONTRIBUTING.md` (~3 lines added: blank line + one-paragraph cross-reference + blank line, inserted between the existing milestone-name paragraph at line 94 and the existing `## Bumping base-image digests` H2 at line 96).
