<objective>
Wave 2 (parallel with Plan 02). Bounds the number of concurrent Tor hidden-service streams the coordinator will serve, using a `tokio::sync::Semaphore` wrapped around the existing accept loop in `coordinator/src/network/tor.rs:75-101`. This is the connection-cap half of Phase 8's DoS resistance — Plan 02 handles per-request rate limiting; this plan handles per-connection admission control at the transport boundary.

Purpose: An attacker who opens unbounded Tor streams to the coordinator can exhaust the OS file-descriptor budget and starve legitimate clients even if every individual stream stays within the rate-limit budget. The semaphore in the accept loop caps the surface at `cfg.coordinator.max_concurrent_connections` per CONTEXT D-04 (default 256). Per A4 resolution, the clearnet path remains uncapped in this phase — clearnet is dev/test-only per CONTEXT; a startup warning tells operators that running clearnet does not enforce the cap.
Output: `network/tor.rs` accept loop with semaphore-gated admission; `run.rs` updated to thread `max_concurrent_connections` into `serve_onion_service` and emit a `tracing::warn!` at startup if `tor_mode = false` clarifying that the cap is tor-only.
</objective>
