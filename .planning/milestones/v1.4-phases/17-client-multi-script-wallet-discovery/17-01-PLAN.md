<objective>
Land WALLET-01: extend `BdkClientWallet` to support three BIP descriptor templates (BIP-84 / BIP-86 / BIP-49) selected by a new `--type` CLI flag, store the script type on the wallet struct as the single source of truth for downstream consumers (17-02 + 17-03), and fail-fast at construction when a user-supplied `--descriptor` disagrees with `--type`.

Purpose: WALLET-01 is the pre-requisite for the WALLET-02 sign dispatcher (17-02 reads `wallet.script_type()`) and the WALLET-03 discovery check (17-03 passes `wallet.script_type()` into `discover_coordinator`). The script-type field on `BdkClientWallet` is also the CRIT-01 wire source for v=2 OwnershipProof envelopes (17-02 writes `script_type: Some(wallet.script_type())`). Coin=0' is preserved across all networks per D-66 so v1.3 wallets continue to derive byte-equivalent addresses (cross-phase invariant).

Output: `client/src/config.rs` with the new `--type` flag + parser, `client/src/wallet.rs` with a `script_type` field on `BdkClientWallet` + per-type descriptor branches in `generate` and `from_descriptor` + the new `script_type()` accessor + a construction-time mismatch check + a script-type line in the FUND-THIS-ADDRESS banner, and `client/src/main.rs` updated to thread `cfg.script_type` into the wallet constructors.
</objective>
